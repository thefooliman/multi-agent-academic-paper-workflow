# Scoring Rubric (Five Dimensions)

How the workflow decides a chapter is "humanized enough". Score each dimension
1–10, weight them, and pass at 42. Below 35, rewrite rather than patch. This
score is the convergence measure alongside the user's own yes/no.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| Concreteness | 1.5 |
| Rhythm | 1.2 |
| Caution | 1.3 |
| Implicit cohesion | 1.0 |
| Researcher voice | 1.0 |

Weighted total = Concreteness×1.5 + Rhythm×1.2 + Caution×1.3 + Cohesion×1.0 +
Voice×1.0. Max 50.

Rhythm thresholds (English): short sentence ≤8 words, long ≥40 words, danger zone
20–30 words.

## Concreteness (1.5×)

Density of checkable specifics: numbers, named authors, cases.

- 1–3: all abstraction, almost nothing you can trace.
- 4–6: a few anchors, but long stretches run on "research shows".
- 7–8: key points have support; occasional filler.
- 9–10: every core claim traces back to data or a source.

Self-check: at least 10 concrete numbers? citations author+year? data sources
named (database, variables, window)?

## Rhythm (1.2×)

Sentence-length variance.

- 1–3: variance <60; five-plus similar-length sentences in a row.
- 4–6: 60–120; some variation, still uniform overall.
- 7–8: 120–180; clear short-long alternation.
- 9–10: >180; a natural spread.

Self-check: one ≤8-word and one ≥40-word sentence per ~200 words? any run of 5+
mid-length sentences? paragraph lengths varied?

## Caution (1.3×)

Epistemic care: hedges, conditions, admitted limits.

- 1–3: "proved / 必然 / 绝对" throughout, no limits.
- 4–6: an occasional hedge, but the main argument stays absolute.
- 7–8: hedges spread out, key conclusions qualified.
- 9–10: actively admits limits, sample boundaries, method constraints, and
  discusses alternatives.

Penalty words: proves, demonstrates, establishes, clearly shows, undoubtedly,
充分说明, 必然导致, 毫无疑问, 绝对, 总是, 从不.
Credit words: suggests, is consistent with, is associated with, may indicate, in
our sample, we cannot rule out, 受限于, 无法识别.

Self-check: at least 5 hedges? at least 2 admitted limits? coefficients phrased
conditionally?

## Implicit cohesion (1.0×)

Paragraphs relay by meaning, not by connectives.

- 1–3: many paragraph-initial Moreover/Furthermore/因此/然而.
- 4–6: occasional, mostly smooth.
- 7–8: almost none; concept overlap carries the transition.
- 9–10: transitions invisible.

Banned paragraph openers: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, 此外, 而且, 并且,
然而, 因此, 所以, 从而, 于是, 进而, 另外. Prefer a direct claim, a question, or a
relay of the prior paragraph's key noun.

## Researcher voice (1.0×)

Does it show a real researcher's stance, choices, and judgment?

- 1–3: "本文/本研究" as subject throughout; reads like a template.
- 4–6: traces of choice, still third-person.
- 7–8: moderate "we", method choice visible.
- 9–10: the reasoning path shows ("we use X rather than Y because…"), trade-offs
  and even a surprise admitted.

Credit: "we use [method] rather than [alternative] because…", "this surprised
us; the theory predicted…", "limited by data, we cannot…".
Penalty: "本文认为…" (overused), "研究表明…" (unnamed), "众所周知…".

Self-check: "we" 2–3 times? researcher choices shown? a surprise admitted?

## Rating

| Total | Grade | Next |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 42 | A | submit |
| 35–41 | B | fix the 2–3 weakest dimensions |
| 28–34 | C | rewrite, don't patch |
| < 28 | D | paragraph-level rewrite |

## Pitfalls

1. Don't fake a "surprise finding" to score; a real researcher discusses a real
   surprise.
2. Don't swap in obscure words to lower perplexity; it damages the seriousness.
3. The score isn't the goal. A high pass rate with a distorted argument is a
   loss; integrity outranks detection score.
