# Default Academic Rules
These rules are enforced for all chapters in write / check mode.

## General academic requirements
1. All factual statements must be reasonable and defensible; do not invent facts, data, citations or historical events without basis.
2. Maintain formal academic tone; avoid colloquial, internet‑slang, overly‑emotional phrasing.
3. Logical flow inside each paragraph shall be coherent; paragraphs should have clear focus.
4. Avoid extreme absolute claims; use appropriate academic hedging where suitable.

## Style & homogeneity rules
1. Prevent excessive stylistic uniformity across the whole paper. Different chapters may naturally vary in sentence rhythm, paragraph length.
2. Avoid heavy over‑use of formulaic opening phrases that are typical of generic AI‑generated text.
3. Paragraph length should vary; do not produce uniformly short or uniformly huge blocks of text.

## Citation & expression
1. If referencing studies, indicate when claims require citation; do not fabricate reference entries.
2. Keep terminology consistent within one chapter.

## Polishing compliance
1. After academic polishing, output must follow polishing‑rules‑en.md.
2. Significant change of core argument of the chapter is not allowed during polishing phase; polishing only optimizes expression, not rewrite core viewpoints.

## Boundary note
This tool targets papers up to 30 pages. Longer manuscripts may contain unnoticed gaps; human full review is always required.

## Academic integrity note
All generated content must be manually reviewed by the user, comply with university academic‑integrity policies.
